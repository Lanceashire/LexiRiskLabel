# DASFAA 2027 Research Track — incremental manuscript revision

Verified 2026-09-05 against https://dasfaa2027.github.io/CallforResearchTrack/list.htm.
Official template: https://link.springer.com/series/558/information-for-authors-and-editors.

- English, double-blind, Springer LNCS (`llncs`, unmodified official class).
- Abstract at most 200 words. Entire PDF at most 16 pages, INCLUDING references and appendices.
- Do not alter margins, body font size, line spacing, or bibliography font to evade the limit.
- Official CFP currently lists submission 2026-11-25; reconfirm before submission.
- Anonymous author/institute fields; no repository owner, machine identifiers or local paths in PDF.
- Incremental revision of the existing paper, NOT fresh literature discovery or model training.
- Reuse verified literature through the existing 2026-09-01 cutoff; metadata checks are not an exhaustive novelty search.
- Policy-level empirical study, not causal decomposition or universal quantity-first optimality.
- Shared split, initial labels, batches, seed and rho do NOT imply shared candidates, budgets or K-star after histories diverge.
- Similar observed counts are not experimental matching. Null intervals are not equivalence or noninferiority evidence.
- Random-order offline replay is not historical-time evaluation; repayment delay is not modeled.
- PPDai and UCI use unit exposure. HMEQ and LendingClub use amount proxies; LGD is one.
- Primary associations use raw differences from NoExplore. Supplemental recovery ratios require positive reference gaps and explicit valid-cell counts.
- New numbers must be traceable to immutable CSV inputs through revision analyses and the experimental-log addendum.
- Repair scientific inputs before PaperOrchestra refinement. Reviewer self-scores are diagnostic, not acceptance probabilities.
- Preserve explicit scientific boundaries as requested by the user, rather than hiding them to improve simulated review scores.
