\paragraph{Resource constraints and predictive evaluation.}
Cost-sensitive active learning already accounts for heterogeneous query costs \cite{donmez2008proactive,tomanek2010cost}. Contextual bandits with knapsack constraints and conservative exploration place learning within resource or baseline-performance restrictions \cite{agrawal2016knapsacks,wu2016conservative}; safety-constrained approaches further consider uncertainty in feasibility \cite{amani2019safe,pacchiano2021linearconstraints}. LRBE uses a point predicted-loss constraint rather than these sequential guarantees. Its secondary scores are evaluated by the resulting data and predictions, not by score values alone. We therefore compare Recall and Brier score while reporting predicted-budget compliance separately from realized defaulted exposure.
